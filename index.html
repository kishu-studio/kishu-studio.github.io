03 The method

How a job runs

The same four steps for a $45 spreadsheet or a month-long build.

  1. Step 01

    Scope

    You describe the result you want. We confirm scope, price and date in writing, usually the same day.

  2. Step 02

    Access

    You invite us as a collaborator or editor. We never ask for your passwords.

  3. Step 03

    Build

    A short written update every working day until it is done.

  4. Step 04

    Handover

    The work, a handover document, and seven days in which fixes are free.
